<details>

<summary>What is "GATA HUB"?</summary>

GATA HUB is the overarching term for all activities in and around GATA. These various ventures of GATA are consisting of the following parts:

* GATA VALIDATORS -> infrastructure services ([validators](/infrastructure/validators/), [relayers](/infrastructure/public-goods/relays/) & [testnet](/infrastructure/validators/testnet/) operations)
* GATA DAO -> DAO activities, core NFT collections ([GATAc](/nfts/genesis-cats/#colonial-cats-gatac) & [GATAv](/nfts/genesis-cats/#voyager-cats-gatav)), [monthly GATA epoch rewards](/nfts/genesis-cats/reward-distribution/)
* GATA YIELD -> Yield series collections ([YGs](/nfts/yield-gorilla/)), [monthly YG epoch rewards](/nfts/yield-gorilla/reward-distribution/)

</details>

<details>

<summary>How does GATA HUB provide value for its community?</summary>

* By operating [validators](/infrastructure/validators/) & distributing their commissions to members of GATA DAO (part of [monthly GATA epoch rewards](/nfts/genesis-cats/reward-distribution/))
* By sharing the royalties from secondary market trading of [GATA NFT collections](/nfts/genesis-cats/) with members of GATA DAO (part of [monthly GATA epoch rewards](/nfts/genesis-cats/reward-distribution/))
* By distributing rewards to holders of Yield series collections ([monthly YG epoch rewards](/nfts/yield-gorilla/reward-distribution/))
* By providing staking rewards to delegators via high quality [infrastructure services](/infrastructure/validators/)
* By having multiple community events throughout the year, in which stakeholders can win rewards from GATA & partner projects (Giveaways, WL spots, NFT drops, Quizzes, Zealy sprints, etc.)

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does "GATA Infrastructure" mean?</summary>

GATA Tech Team is handling all activities under the umbrella term GATA VALIDATORS. These are all activities that are related to the setup & ongoing operations of

* [GATA validators](/infrastructure/validators/)
* [GATA relayers](/infrastructure/public-goods/relays/)
* [Participation in Testnets](/infrastructure/validators/testnet/)
* [ICS Partner Chains](/cosmos/interchain-security/)
* [Foundation Delegations](/infrastructure/validators/foundation-delegations/)

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does "GATA DAO" mean?</summary>

GATA DAO is the decision making group of people within GATA HUB and everyone who holds a [GATAc](/nfts/genesis-cats/#colonial-cats-gatac) and/or [GATAv](/nfts/genesis-cats/#voyager-cats-gatav) NFT is eligible to take part in that group.

This group navigates GATA HUB's ventures by democratic decision making - performed by discussing current & future activities in GATA Discord and by finalizing decisions via onchain voting.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does "GATA YIELD" mean?</summary>

All Yield Series NFT collections fall under the term GATA YIELD, for example [Yield Gorillas (YG)](/nfts/yield-gorilla/). These NFT collections provide their holders with [monthly YG epoch rewards](/nfts/yield-gorilla/reward-distribution/) which are based on the performance of the mint funds that are partially getting staked to GATA validators and used in various DeFi activities managed by the GATA team.

You can find more info on the concept of Yield series NFTs in the section [YG & Yield Concept](/nfts/yield-gorilla/yield-concept/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>How often do you distribute rewards to holders of GATA/YG NFTs?</summary>

Rewards get distributed at the beginning of each month (one month = one epoch) and separately for

* GATA DAO in form of [monthly GATA epoch rewards](/nfts/genesis-cats/reward-distribution/) and for
* GATA YIELD in form of [monthly YG rewards](/nfts/yield-gorilla/reward-distribution/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>I'm holding a Yield series NFT - can I join GATA DAO with that?</summary>

No, only holders of [Colonial Cats (GATAc)](/nfts/genesis-cats/#colonial-cats-gatac) and [Voyager Cats (GATAv)](/nfts/genesis-cats/#voyager-cats-gatav) are eligible to participate in the activities of GATA DAO and get [monthly GATA epoch rewards](/nfts/genesis-cats/reward-distribution/).

However, with your Yield series NFT you're still eligible for receiving [monthly YG epoch rewards](/nfts/yield-gorilla/reward-distribution/).

</details>

<summary>How do GATA distribute rewards?</summary>

To enable a smoother experience for everyone, we have introduced a new streamlined distribution method for community rewards by using Omniflix campaigns enhanced by Streampay, winners of community activities & events;\

• Will get NFTs airdropped to them on Omniflix \
• These NFTs can get burned via [Omniflix campaigns](https://omniflix.market/campaigns/all) \
• The winners get their reward tokens streamed directly to their wallet via [Streampay](https://streampay.me/) tech
